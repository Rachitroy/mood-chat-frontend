## Mockup Options to Generate

We'll create 3 distinct visual directions for Mood Chat:

### Option 1: Modern Glassmorphism Premium
**Style:** Sleek, transparent layers, depth, premium feel
**Key Elements:**
- Frosted glass sidebar
- Smooth gradients
- Subtle shadows and glows
- Floating elements
- Elegant emotion indicators
- Colors: Deep purples, blues, pinks with transparency

### Option 2: Clean Minimalist Light
**Style:** Simple, spacious, content-focused
**Key Elements:**
- Lots of white space
- Clean typography
- Subtle emotion colors
- Simple borders
- Light background with colored accents
- Colors: Whites, soft grays, pastel emotion colors

### Option 3: Dark Immersive Emotional
**Style:** Deep, cinematic, emotion-forward
**Key Elements:**
- Rich black backgrounds
- Vibrant emotion colors that pop
- Subtle neon glows
- Dramatic depth
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Colors: Deep blacks, vibrant emotion colors, subtle glows
